# Changelog

## Unreleased
- F22 groundwork for the drug page: `pairing.py` links each drug screen to untreated, full-genome screens of the same cell line, labelled by match level (same study and library, same study, or another study on the same cell line; at most 3 of the last kind). Unmatched drug screens are kept with match "none". `export_site.py` writes `drug_pairs.json` and adds counts to `summary.json`. On the ORCS 2.0.18 index: 484 drug screens, best match same study and library 63, same cell line only 348, none 73.
- M3 recipe 2 (method approved by Richard 2026-10-11): on `/gene`, "Does it depend on a mutation?" compares the gene's essential-scale values between lines with and without a mutation in a second gene. One value per line (median of its screens), difference of medians with a seeded bootstrap 95% interval, two-sided Mann-Whitney test, at least 5 lines per group; flagged lines and lines without mutation calls are left out (`src/scripts/recipe.ts`). Independent SciPy reference test in CI; manual "Validate recipes" workflow checks known biology (KRAS, BRAF, PIK3CA, TP53, MDM2) on staging data. `lines.json` gains `mutation_calls`.
- F20/F21: drug and other treatment names now reach the site. `export_site.py` joins the `conditions` table into `screens.json` (`treatment`, `treatment_dose`, `treatment_duration`); `/data` and `/gene` get a Treatment column, a Treatment filter and treatment search; `/data` also gets a Setup filter (drug, virus, timecourse...). Needs one re-run of "Publish data to R2 staging".
- M3b step 3: cell line context on the pages (`src/scripts/context.ts`). `/data` gains a Tissue filter, a "Key mutations in line" column (driver panel) and mutation search; `/gene` shows, per screen, the tissue and the looked-up gene's own mutation, expression and copy number in that line, with filters. Plain-language bands for expression and copy number are display aids, not statistical calls. CC BY 4.0 credit for DepMap and Cellosaurus. Pages work unchanged if the context is not published.
- M3b step 2: cell line tables from DepMap 24Q4 (signatures, protein-altering mutations, expression, copy number) for the screened lines; per-gene JSON shards; manual workflow "Publish cell line context to R2 staging".
- M3b step 1: cell line context pipeline (`datasets/cell-line-context/`): matches every ORCS screen's cell line to Cellosaurus (and through it to DepMap), never guessing ambiguous names; GitHub job downloads the sources, records licence evidence and reports coverage. Publishes nothing.
- M3 quick win (F4, F20, F21): the screens table on `/data` and the results table on `/gene` get filters (cell line, study, library, phenotype, selection, comparability, hit), a text search, column sorting and 25/50/100-row pages (`src/scripts/table.ts`). New plain-language key to the scores (`ScoreExplainer`) on both pages.
