The test is the one already approved for the two-group comparison. New, not part of the approved method: a column "p times 3", a plain allowance for the three comparisons, labelled as a caution. Classes as in the precomputed associations; lines with both classes, flagged lines and lines without mutation calls are left out. Link form: `/gene?g=X&by=mutclass&y=Y`.
