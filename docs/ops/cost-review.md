# Monthly cost review (10 minutes)

Put this in your calendar for the first of each month.

1. Cloudflare: open Billing. Is the amount 0 (apart from the domain)? Check R2 storage and operations against the free tier.
2. Cloudflare analytics: any traffic spike? Unusual countries or bots?
3. GitHub: Actions minutes used. Spending limit still 0?
4. Sanity: still on the free plan?
5. Payment card: statement shows only expected charges (domain, subscriptions you chose)?
6. Note the numbers below. If any service exceeds 50% of its free tier, tell the agent and decide what to change.
